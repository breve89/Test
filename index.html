How the flags work

Each player shows season stats, this week's opponent and their win odds, injury status from the reports in Fan HQ, and recent news that mentions them.

  • Bench: ruled out, on IR, or on a bye.
  • Monitor: questionable or doubtful; check before kickoff.
  • Start: no injury flags and playing this week.

Injury reports cover the Patriots and their opponent; other teams rely on news mentions. This is a guide, not a guarantee.
